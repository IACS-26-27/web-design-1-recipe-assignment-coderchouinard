Salsa Verde Recipe

Ingredients

  • Tomatillos: You'll need about x12 of these
  • Diced Onions: You need a 1/2 cup of these
  • Garlic: You will need x3 cloves
  • Cilantro: 1/2 of a cup will work great
  • Green chilie: You'll need x1 of these.

Recipe Info

This recipe serves 8 people and takes about 15 minutes of prep and 6 minutes of cooking time. While it can be hard to find the ingidients its a quick and lovely tart salsa dish that goes good with almost anything.

Instructions

  1. Broil the tomatillos, garlic, and greenchilie pepper. Broiling the tomatillos, garlic, and chile pepper for a few minutes adds some beaughtifull looking and tasting char-roasted look, and softens the tomatillos a bit so they blend easily.
  2. Rinse the onions. To make sure its not to stronge, rinse them under cold water before blending.
  3. Blend. The nice part about a blender is it does all the hard work. Put in the broiled veggies, the rinsed onions, cilantro, salt, and a spash of water (salsa verde thickens up when chilled, so the water keeps it at a good consistency). You can adjust the consistency to what you like.
